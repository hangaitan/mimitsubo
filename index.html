What is mimitsubo?

Your ear is a tiny map. For centuries, East Asian traditions have pressed points on the ear that are linked to different parts of the body. In the 1950s a French doctor drew the ear map the West still uses today — and in Japan the idea became something gentler: a tiny seed on a patch, worn like jewellery and pressed a few times a day.

03

Applying steps

  1. 1
    Clean

    Wipe your ear with the cleansing wipe and let it dry fully — a trace of oil or sweat is enough to stop a patch from sticking.

  2. 2
    Find

    Move the copper pen slowly over the area until you find the point you want.

  3. 3
    Place

    Lift one patch and press it onto the point, seed against the skin.

  4. 4
    Wear

    Press each seed softly for about 30 seconds, a few times a day. Take the patch off after three to five days.

Tip · 3–5 days per ear, then rest & switch.
