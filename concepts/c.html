What is mimitsubo?

Your ear is a tiny map. For centuries, East Asian traditions have pressed points on the ear that are linked to different parts of the body. In the 1950s a French doctor, Paul Nogier, drew the ear map the West still uses today.

In Japan the idea became something playful: small seeds or 24k gold pellets on a clear patch, worn like ear jewellery and pressed a few times a day. It is now one of the most loved self-care rituals in Tokyo — and it is starting to bloom in Seoul, too.
